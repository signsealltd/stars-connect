# V1.17.0 - Previous timesheets

Timesheets now has From/To date selection and Today, Yesterday, Last week and This month shortcuts. Historical staff summaries expand to daily clock events and recorded hours, including correction indicators and missing clock-outs. Archived staff with clocking activity in the selected range remain visible.

Past periods are read-only. Today's manual clocking workflow remains available. Links lead to saved payroll/timesheet PDFs and payroll runs. Existing payroll snapshots and audit records are unchanged; no migration is required.

Dates use Europe/London boundaries. Ranges are limited to one year per view, with no restriction on how far back users can look. Hours use paired daily events; missing clock-outs are not estimated or joined to an unrelated later day. These recorded hours do not replace approved payroll totals.
