# V1.13.1

Previous version: V1.13.0.

- Tasks now offers Mark complete, Delete task, and Reopen/Restore for authorised operational, billing reminder, premises and compliance records. Deletion archives records; Deleted retains the audit trail. Notes and reasons are recorded atomically with the underlying change.
- Shared task records drive dashboard counts and calendar views. Open tabs refresh after task changes or on focus. Deleted billing reminders are not regenerated by existing upserts. Completing a reminder does not mark invoices paid.
- Clinical medication, fleet safety and staff approval workflows retain their dedicated transitions. Evidence and independent verification remain enforced for compliance completion.
- Removed medication stock summary from dashboard; medication remains under People.
- Moved Portal access to Settings, removed Staff portal header group and duplicate Tasks menu link. Tasks remains on dashboard.

No schema migration or historical record deletion. Archived tasks can be restored. Existing staff requests are not bulk-completed or removed.

Validation: lifecycle, queue and navigation tests; browser/database validation of completion, archive, restore, stale-status rejection, calendar visibility and audit persistence; TypeScript and production build.
