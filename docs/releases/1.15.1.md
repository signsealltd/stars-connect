# V1.15.1 — Invoice deletion

Previous version: V1.15.0. Previous invoices now has Delete on each row and Delete selected for up to 100 invoices, with a confirmation and audit reason. Billing edit and approval permissions are required.

Deletion marks invoices DELETED instead of removing financial history. The normal invoice list and download selection exclude them. Payments already selects only issued, void and superseded invoices, so deleted invoices disappear from Outstanding, Paid and Ignored lists and their summary totals. Existing PDFs and payment events are retained; payment revisions increment to reject stale payment actions. Invoice numbers are not reused. An already-open Payments page refreshes when returning to it or when another tab deletes invoices.

Current batch exports are invalidated and regenerated without deleted invoices. Older export documents remain superseded for audit. No schema migration is required. No production invoices were deleted during verification.

Checked with automated tests, local browser/API verification of outstanding and paid deletions, unchanged payment events, idempotent retries, audit entries, download rejection, input validation and unauthenticated denial; production build also checked.
