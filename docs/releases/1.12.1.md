# V1.12.1 — Staff grade management access

Settings-created Team Leader and Support Worker accounts could retain standalone
role defaults rather than follow the configured Access Level. This caused the
dashboard to return 403 and send them to the legacy staff portal.

Deploy migration `20260924153000_link_legacy_staff_grade_accounts` before starting
the new build. It links unassigned accounts in these two unambiguous roles to
their matching grade and replaces their legacy permission snapshots with that
grade's configured permissions. Existing explicit access-level links, administrators,
managers, staff data and PINs remain unchanged. Review the configured grades before
deployment: their permissions become authoritative for these previously unlinked accounts.

New Settings-created accounts in these roles are also linked. Session resolution
supports legacy unlinked accounts and reloads configured permissions on each request.
Missing explicitly assigned or inactive levels fail closed. Staff PIN sessions
remain separate from management login.
